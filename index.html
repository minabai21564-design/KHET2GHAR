    How it works

    1. Register

      Farmers and buyers create an account with their name, phone number and address.

    2. List crops

      Farmers add price, quantity and category for what they have ready.

    3. Find and match

      Buyers search and filter by category, price or location.

    4. Order securely

      Buyers pay by UPI, card or net banking. The farmer is notified.
